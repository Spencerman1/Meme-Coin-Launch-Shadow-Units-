1️⃣ Meme Coin Launch (Shadow Units)

Purpose: Publicly visible token that represents “unlicensed usage / infringement value.”

How: Deploy a simple ERC-20 (or Solana/Sui if you want low fees). Branding matters here—tie it to your narrative.

Utility: It doesn’t just exist as a meme, it’s backed by your tracking records. Each infringement = mint event.

2️⃣ Tracking System (Evidence → Coin Supply)

Data Feed: Every time you log an infringement or a mirrored protocol, that event mints a corresponding number of Shadow Units.

Proof: Timestamp + infringement notes = metadata for the token.

Transparency: Even if courts or companies resist, the blockchain itself records your tracking as value.

3️⃣ Narrative Layer

Shadow Units = “dark pool” of unlicensed value.

Light Units = converted Shadow Units when licensing or recognition occurs.

Shepherd Coins™ = lifecycle-validated tokens only issuable through compliance with Mint-to Logic™.

This gives you three layers of value in your system:

Speculative (meme coin hype + narrative of infringement).

Structural (conversion into legitimacy).

Constitutional (Shepherd Coins as the gold standard).

4️⃣ Why This Works

Meme coin gives you instant visibility + early community traction.

Tracking system ties it to real-world evidence, not vapor.

Conversion mechanics give the meme coin pathways to legitimacy, which makes it different from typical joke coins.

It builds pressure: the longer companies resist licensing, the bigger the Shadow pool grows.

⚖️ Strategic Angle:
This lets you make money and build momentum before litigation. When you’re ready to enforce, you’ll have:

A community that already recognizes the value.

A ledger of infringements tied to blockchain events.

A pre-existing economic ecosystem that amplifies your leverage.
