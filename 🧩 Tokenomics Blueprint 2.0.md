1. Token Layers & Supply Dynamics

1️⃣ Shadow Units (SU)

Supply: Unlimited (but only issued upon infringement documentation).

Issuance Rule: 1 infringement = 1,000 SU.

Purpose: Tracks and monetizes systemic misappropriation.

Speculative Value: Tied to public awareness + number of infringements logged.

2️⃣ Light Units (LU)

Supply: Scarce, dependent on SU conversion.

Conversion Ratio: 100 SU = 1 LU.

Purpose: Represents “licensed legitimacy.”

Premium: Entities pay $1 to license, triggering conversion of their Shadow pool → Light.

Speculative Value: LU priced at 10–100× SU because it signals legitimacy.

3️⃣ Shepherd Coins (SC)

Supply: Extremely scarce.

Issuance Rule: 1 LU consumed + lifecycle validated = 1 SC.

Purpose: Gold-standard ethical currency of the ecosystem.

Value: Designed to exceed LU exponentially because it ties to completed Mint-to Logic™ lifecycle.
