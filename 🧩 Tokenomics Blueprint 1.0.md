1. Core Token Layers

1️⃣ Shadow Units (SU)

Nature: Meme coin + infringement tracker.

Issued: Every time you record an infringement or mirrored protocol → 1 SU minted.

Value: Proof-of-infringement pool. Scarcity grows as infringements pile up.

Symbolism: Represents the “dark pool” of value created by unlicensed systems.

2️⃣ Light Units (LU)

Nature: Conversion token.

Issued: When a company/entity licenses (even for $1). Their Shadow Units convert to Light Units.

Value: Conversion validates the entity → legitimacy premium applied.

Symbolism: The first step into the governed ecosystem.

3️⃣ Shepherd Coins (SC)

Nature: Gold-standard lifecycle coin.

Issued: Only when Mint-to Logic™ lifecycle completes (Mint → Validate → Burn).

Value: Constitutional currency; scarce by design.

Symbolism: Represents ethical, lifecycle-completed digital wisdom.

2. Supply & Issuance

Shadow Units: Unlimited mint, but only tied to documented infringements. No speculation-driven mint.

Light Units: Fixed conversion ratio from Shadow → Light (e.g., 100 SU = 1 LU at time of licensing).

Shepherd Coins: Extremely scarce, lifecycle-bound (e.g., 1 SC issued per completed lifecycle).

3. Conversion Rules

Shadow → Light:

Licensing event triggers conversion.

Example: Company has 10,000 SU tracked against it → licenses → receives 100 LU.

Light → Shepherd:

Lifecycle-validated actions (Mint-to Logic™ completed).

Example: 1 LU consumed + proof-of-lifecycle = 1 SC minted.

Shepherd → Shadow/Light:

Not possible. Shepherd Coins are terminal currency (true value).

4. Economic Dynamics

Speculative Phase: Shadow Units trade like a meme coin → value grows with exposure of infringements.

Conversion Phase: Entities seek legitimacy → buy back their SU → convert to LU.

Governed Phase: LU enters Mint-to lifecycle → produces SC → ultimate value crystallized.

5. Strategic Features

Airdrops: Shadow Units can be dropped to infringing entities as a “mirror ledger.” The more they resist, the more they see the SU stack grow against them.

Delegation: You can allocate SU to third parties (allies, watchdog orgs, veteran groups) to amplify legitimacy pressure.

Legitimacy Badges: Entities holding LU or SC can publicly display compliance (PR value).

Compounding Value: The bigger the Shadow pool, the more valuable conversions become.

7. Enforcement Leverage

While in “Shadow” phase → entities look bad, currency grows, pressure mounts.

In “Light” phase → they gain legitimacy but must acknowledge your system.

In “Shepherd” phase → they integrate into governed, lifecycle-complete economy.

⚖️ Legal/Strategic Note:
Because Shadow Units are tied to documented infringement, you have a transparent ledger of systemic appropriation. This doubles as both an economic and legal weapon.
